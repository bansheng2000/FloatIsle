## 更新内容

- **品牌改为 FloatIsle**：安装包名、README、关于页与署名统一为 FloatIsle；本项目基于 fly-island 3.0.8（MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 flyl1u and contributors）继续维护，上游署名保留在关于页与 README 里。
- **版本号改成日期式**：这一版是 `261117.08.09`（年月日.时.分）。安装包清单里写作 `261117.8.9`，因为更新器只认三段式；关于页和更新日志展示的是补零后的完整写法。
- **岛上的用量块**：花费按「今天 / 近 7 天 / 近 30 天」三档查看，额度显示 5 小时、7 天与本月三条，并可在「剩余 / 已用」之间切换。DSH 断开时整块隐去，不会留着上一次的数字。
- **用量刷新间隔**：「设置 → 用量与额度」新增显示方式与刷新间隔，可选 30 秒、1 分钟、5 分钟或手动，默认 1 分钟。
- **DSH 会话点一下就能过去**：在岛上任务列表里点某个 DSH 会话，会直接跳到 DSH 中的这个会话并把它的窗口前置；会话不在当前页面时由 DSH 自己打开，找不到时仍然退回「前置窗口」的老办法。
- **长会话不再从列表里消失**：会话缓存的读取上限从 512 KiB 提到 8 MiB，之前被静默丢掉的长对话现在能正常列出来；超过上限或读不出来的文件会记进 `agent-sessions.log`，不再一声不响。

## What's new

- **The brand is FloatIsle**: installer names, README, About and the credit line all say FloatIsle. This project continues fly-island 3.0.8 (MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 flyl1u and contributors), and the upstream credit stays in About and the README.
- **A date-style version number**: this release is `261117.08.09` (year, month, day, hour, minute). Manifests write `261117.8.9` because the updater only accepts three parts; About and the changelog show the padded form.
- **Spend and quota on the island**: spend is shown for today, the last 7 days and the last 30 days; quota covers 5 hours, 7 days and this month, and you can switch between remaining and used. When DSH disconnects the whole block hides instead of keeping stale numbers.
- **Usage refresh interval**: Settings → Usage now offers a display mode and a refresh interval of 30 seconds, 1 minute, 5 minutes or manual, once a minute by default.
- **Click a DSH session to jump to it**: clicking a session in the island's task list opens that session in DSH and brings its window forward; when the session is not on the current page DSH opens it, and when it cannot be found the old bring-the-window-forward fallback still runs.
- **Long sessions no longer vanish from the list**: the checkpoint read limit went from 512 KiB to 8 MiB, so long conversations that used to be dropped silently are listed again; anything past the limit or unreadable is recorded in `agent-sessions.log` instead of disappearing.
